How this plan was sequenced

  • Durations are the working-day estimates from the readiness sheet. Each person works one task at a time; independent items run in parallel across owners.
  • Security roles (81 custom roles, taken from the org's unmanaged roles) run first over 2 days in four groups, then app-wise configuration and testing per job profile over 2 days across the 22 custom apps (xr_ prefix). Click any row to expand. Child tables for Lead and Opportunity are the xr_ tables with a lookup to them, read from the org.
  • Sanskar's automation flows (46 in total) are grouped as listed by the team and sit inside the sheet's original durations (notifications 1 day, approvals 2 days, Chanakya 2 days). Every email flow logs its action in the xr_email table. 19 notification flows in 1 day is tight and may need more time.
  • Blob upload controls also start first. Edge-case testing follows the build items; user manuals follow the dashboards.
  • Data migration starts Tue 22 Sep: Masters, BD, Inventory, Sourcing, Lead, Site Visit, Opportunity, with Chanakya tables standalone after Inventory. Lead moved ahead of Site Visit because the visit table looks up the lead. Prospect runs last (6L+ rows, estimated 3 days, up from 2 for 5L), so its lookups from Lead and Visit are filled in afterwards. Closing Manager feedback follows Lead. Click the row to expand.
  • Dependency alerts: dashboards need the KPI definitions finalised; the BD migration needs billing schedule and agreement data for all projects.
